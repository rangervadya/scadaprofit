Workflow:
A plumber uploads a source photo to a street/house inbox. An office user opens that exact scheme and clicks Convert. After analysis, the app automatically creates/reuses an address-linked LPGCad R1 CAD project and shows its sheet preview inside the scheme record. Edit opens that same project in Manual CAD and provides a return link to the house scheme.

Safety: only selected primary CAD edges and separately ink-validated Qwen paths are imported as geometry. Review-only lines, uncertain crossings, unknown symbols, unconfirmed dimensions, and apartment/riser assignments are not invented. Incomplete analyses are marked as drafts requiring review.
