Changes:
- New Manual CAD projects default to LPGCad R1. The appendix label is editable.
- LPGCad R1 footer reserves separate areas for legend, wrapped notes and signatures in both SVG and DXF.
- A graph crossing marked needs_review suppresses the automatically inferred junction marker at that location.
- OCR and Qwen route-tracing use adaptive 2x2 tiling for ordinary images (long side <=1600 px and short side <=1200 px); larger scans retain the configured grid.
- Legacy default timeouts are raised internally (OCR 180->240 s; PIPE 240->300 s); one transient retry is enabled. Set QWEN_TILE_RETRY_BUDGET=off to disable retries.
- House archive workflow remains in place: source photo -> explicit office conversion -> address-linked LPGCad R1 project -> Manual CAD.

Safety: only selected primary CAD edges and ink-validated Qwen routes are promoted. Unknown symbols, unconfirmed measurements, apartment lists, and ambiguous geometry are not invented. Incomplete analysis remains a draft for human review.
